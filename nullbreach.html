Why you can trust these numbers
  • Re-run any time — the code checks are deterministic; the AI pass may phrase things slightly differently between runs.
  • Package vulnerabilities come live from api.osv.dev at scan time — nothing cached, nothing guessed.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Your code never touches our servers — there are no Nullbreach servers.
  • The JSON export carries every finding with its location and fix, ready for your issue tracker.

“A strong first pass — not a substitute for a professional audit.”
